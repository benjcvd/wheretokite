# Release 1.1: upload and submit

Archive: Xcode → Window → Organizer → **WhereToKite 1.1 (3)**, 18 Nov 2026, built from main 92783fc.

## 1. Upload (5 min)

1. In Organizer, select **WhereToKite 1.1 (3)** → **Distribute App** → **App Store Connect** → **Upload** → keep the defaults → **Upload**.
2. Wait for Apple's email "has completed processing" (10–30 min).

## 2. Create the version (5 min)

1. [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com) → WhereToKite → **iOS App +** (left column, next to 1.0) → `1.1`.
2. **Build** → **+** → pick build **3**.
3. Paste **What's New** below, once per language (language menu at the top right).
4. Everything else carries over from 1.0. Screenshots can stay for now: they show the 1.0 layout.
5. **Add for Review** → **Submit**. Choose automatic release if you want it live as soon as Apple approves it.

## What's New

### English (U.S.)

```
Wind first.
• Hour by hour: one clear line per hour with wind, gusts and direction. When gusts alone are over your limit, the gust number is the one in red.
• Wind chart: switch between your kites and the colours follow the kite you pick.
• Choose flat or wavy water, and good or any weather.
• New search screen: rider, start point, drive time and conditions in four tiles.
• Non-kiteable spots say why in one word: offshore, too light, storm…
• Directions open in Apple Maps or Google Maps.
• Hundreds of spots checked again across Europe, plus new ones in Italy, Greece, Germany, the Netherlands, Portugal and Turkey.
• Fixes: a failed search no longer shows the previous answer; start points outside Europe and Morocco get a clear message.
```
