# Validation: South Finistère, Crozon to Bénodet (fr-finistere-s, WHE-113)

Done 5 Nov 2026. Windy browsed as a visitor (hover on the kite markers, names and stars only); pins come
from OSM, never from Windy. Few kite schools or town pages exist for this coast, so most sources are
spot guides (kite-and-windsurfing-guide, kiteforum), the Brittany tourism board, beach directories and
school listings. No decree or town-hall page on kites was found, so nothing is encoded in `banned`.
New duplicate rule applied (Lead, 5 Nov): under 1 km, same water, same facing only.

## Spots

| Spot | Tier | Verdict | What was checked and sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pentrez | basic | ok, notes added | OSM beach way, faces WSW (Apple Maps agrees). Beach directories (plages.tv, bord-de-mer.com) list a kite zone; West Gliss kite school in Saint-Nic; kite guide: waves, sideshore in S to SW winds, low tide gives room to ride on sand. Windy marker (4). No tide window encoded: sources only say low tide gives more sand. |
| La Torche | basic | fixed (pin) | Pin was a text search; now the OSM beach way "Plage de la Torche", 700 m north of the point, faces W (Apple Maps agrees), which matches kiteforum: "walk to the north side of the point, where kiters launch". Kite schools Twentynine and CEKB (Plomeur, Pays Bigouden tourist office listings; CEKB also teaches at Tronoën); kiteforum: intermediate to expert, currents. Windy marker (5). Local signs mentioned in notes; no kite decree found. Tronoën (1.5 km north, kite zone 300 m south of the car park) in notes. |
| Mousterlin | basic | ok, notes rewritten | Pin on "Plage de Mousterlin" (now noted as west of the point), faces SW, 10 m from the shore. Brittany tourism board lists Mousterlin among five kite spots of Brittany; plages.tv shows a kite zone; an association teaches kite there (cparici guide, commercial). The guide page "Kerambigorn" is Beg-Meil, 3 km east, a different beach: not used for this row. Not on Windy. |
| Crozon – Goulien | pro | ok, notes rewritten | OSM named beach, faces W. Surf and wind guides (vvanat, plages.tv): crescent of the Anse de Dinan, sheltered from swell, fallback to La Palue, NE wind for wave kite, avoid W wind with big swell, rocks at low tide. No Windy marker under this name (Kersiguénou is 1.1 km away, added). Kept Pro. |
| Île-Tudy | pro | ok, notes rewritten | OSM relation Grande Plage du Sillon, faces SE. Kite guide: rocks mid-beach make a standing-depth lagoon at low tide, waves and shorebreak with strong S wind at high tide. Tourist office: lifeguards 1 July to 31 August, 13:00 to 19:00. The "banned 12:00 to 19:00 in summer" in the old notes had no source (Pays Bigouden municipal decrees PDF is a scanned image, could not be read); worded as reported, check the signs. Windy marker (4). |
| Bénodet – Lagune du Letty | pro | fixed (notes) | Kite guide, Brittany tourism and a vvanat page: works with southerly winds and around high water only, strong currents, go with an instructor (Natura 2000 site). Old notes said "best in S to E winds" and tide preference "on the flood": rewritten to the sources. Different water from Mousterlin so not a duplicate. Not on Windy. **Open issue**: see TO_CONFIRM. |
