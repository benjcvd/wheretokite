# Windy markers the old 2 km rule kept out: Landes to Morbihan (WHE-131)

Done 5 Nov 2026. New rule (README, 5 Nov): launches under 1 km apart, same water and same facing, are one spot; anything else stays at any distance. Windy markers left out for "< 2 km" in the reports of Landes / Pays basque, Gironde, Charente and Morbihan were re-read under it. Sources are the web pages named below; pins come from OSM names (never Windy). Windy was not re-browsed: marker names and stars are from the zone reports.

## Result

| Zone | Left out for the 2 km rule | Now added (Pro) | Still left out (new reason) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landes / Pays basque (`fr-basque-landes`) | 1 (Hossegor) | 1 | 0 |
| Gironde (`fr-gironde`) | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Charente (`fr-charente`) | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Morbihan (`fr-morbihan`) | 9 | 5 | 4 |

Gironde and Charente: no marker was left out for distance (Charente's Banc du Bucheron is the same sandbank as Les Portes, 1.3 km but same water and facing, and it was matched, not skipped).

## Counts

- Markers re-read: 10 Windy markers left out for distance (Hossegor, Larmor Plage, and 8 on the Carnac / Plouharnel coast: Carnac Plage, Légénèse, Sainte-Barbe, Les Crevettes, Mane Gwen, Port d'Orange, plus Saint-Colomban from the TO_CONFIRM list; Saint-Colomban has no separate Windy line in the report).
- Added as Pro: 6 (Hossegor, Toulhars, Carnac Grande Plage, Saint-Colomban, Mané Guen, Sainte-Barbe). Left out with a listed reason: 3 (Légénèse, Les Crevettes, Port d'Orange).
- Catalogue FR-atlantic: 98 → 104 spots, all Pro. Spot check: no PROBLEMS, the 6 new spots all `agrees` with Apple Maps.

## Risks

- Carnac: the summer ban comes from guides, not a town order; rows say "check before riding".
- Mané Guen and Sainte-Barbe are exposed ocean beaches (swell). Notes say so.
