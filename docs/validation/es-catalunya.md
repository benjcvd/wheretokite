# Validation: Catalonia (es-catalunya)

Cards WHE-246 and WHE-247 (same job), 21 Nov 2026. Method: [validation README](../../tools/spots/validation/README.md).

## Past work (not redone)
Sourced checks already on record, kept as they are (see [TO_CONFIRM](../../tools/spots/TO_CONFIRM.md#catalunya-catalan-rows-of-escsv)):
Barcelona to Ebro delta (WHE-66, 1 Nov, Castelldefels council hours, delta notes), Costa Brava / Empordà
(WHE-65, 1 Nov, notes from tourism pages, L'Estartit re-pinned), pins (WHE-59), Windy read (WHE-83, 1 Nov),
settled questions (WHE-2, 2 Nov), pin and direction pass (WHE-244, 21 Nov: all Catalan directions `agrees` with
Apple Maps; the pins 100–270 m inland are fixed by the build change `SNAP_IF_FARTHER` 300 → 100).

What is new here: two things changed since those passes. (1) On 4 Nov the rule became "a Windy kite marker
we lack is added as Pro unless a listed reason applies", so the three markers left out in WHE-83 and the
13 spots archived in bulk on 16 Nov (WHE-213, "no independent source", no new research) are re-judged.
(2) A **2026 list of the summer kite zones** was found: [wearerbels.com, Zonas Kite Cataluña 2026](https://wearerbels.com/zonas-kite-catalunya.html)
(a school-side guide, not an official text, but specific and consistent with the council pages already used).
It names the only places where kiting is allowed from 1 Jun to 30 Sep: La Rubina, Sant Pere Pescador
(Piscifactoria channel EN6 and ION Club channel EN11), L'Estartit, Malgrat de Mar, Castelldefels, plus
Riumar and El Trabucador (described as open all year). Every other Catalan beach has no summer zone, which
matches the summer bans we already carry. Treated as a second source, never as the base of a `banned`.
